== Malai Chicken
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: dinner

- 1 | chicken cut into pieces | chicken
- 3 Tbsp | oil for onion | vegetable oil
- 1 tsp | ginger | ginger
- 1 tsp | garlic | garlic
- 2 tsp | chilli powder | chili powder
- 1/2 tsp | turmeric | turmeric
- 1/2 tsp | garam masala | garam masala
- 1/2 tsp | zeera | cumin
- 1 tsp | salt | salt
- 1 tsp | salt (gravy) | salt
- 4 | med onions (2 Tbsp oil fry / heat it a little bit) (gravy) | onions
- 6 | tomatoes (1/2 kg) (gravy) | tomatoes
- 1/4 tsp | turmeric (gravy) | turmeric
- 1/4 tsp | garam masala (gravy) | garam masala
- 1/4 tsp | zeera (gravy) | cumin
- 1 tsp | chilli (gravy) | chili powder
- 1 bunch | corriander (gravy) | fresh coriander
- 3 | green chillies (gravy) | green chilies
- 500 g | malai (to bake with) | cream
- | eggs (on top after 15 mins) | eggs
- | butter (optional) | butter
> After frying the onions put the chicken and all of the spices and cook till a bit tender.
> Gravy: Boil, peel and grind the tomatoes.
> Add this to the fried onion, then add the spices.
> Dont cook till dry.
> Put this gravy and chicken in oven tray and put 500 g of malai and bake for 1/2 an hour.
> Add eggs on top after 15 mins and some butter.
> If using fresh malai dont use butter.
