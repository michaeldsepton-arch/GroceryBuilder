== Instant Marag for Kabab (Turkish)
cuisine: Turkish | meal: side
- 1 Tbsp | Garlic | garlic
- 1 | Maggie cube | chicken bouillon cube
- 2-3 Tbsp | Tomato paste | tomato paste
- 1/2-1 cup | Water | water
- | Oil | vegetable oil
> Saute garlic in little oil, add maggie cube then tomato paste.
> Saute for 1-2 mins then add water and cook on low for 5-7 mins.
