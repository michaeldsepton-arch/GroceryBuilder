== Keema Aloo w/Peas
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: dinner
- 1/2 Kg | Mince meat | ground meat
- 1 | Onion, diced | onions
- 2 | Tomatoes, pureed | tomatoes
- | Oil | cooking oil
- 5 | Cardamom pods | cardamom pods
- 1/2 | Stick cinnamon | cinnamon sticks
- 4 | Long/cloves | whole cloves
- 1 Tbsp | Kasuri methi | kasuri methi
- 1/2 cup | Frozen peas | frozen peas
- | Fresh dhania to garnish | cilantro
- 1 tsp | Red chili powder (spices) | red chili powder
- 1.5 tsp | Coriander powder (spices) | coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp | Cumin powder (spices) | cumin
- 1/2 tsp | Turmeric powder (spices) | turmeric
- 2 tsp | Garlic | garlic
- 1 tsp | Ginger | ginger
- 1/2 tsp | Garam masala (spices) | garam masala
- 1 | Potato, diced | potatoes
> Saute onion in oil along with whole garam masala.
> Add ginger, garlic and saute for 1-2 mins.
> Add the rest of the spices and cook for 1 min.
> Add the rest of the ingredients and cook for 20-25 mins.
> Turn off heat and add peas and fresh coriander.
> Note: Add potatoes in the beginning along with the onions to soften and cook faster.
