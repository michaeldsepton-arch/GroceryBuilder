== Gajar ka Halwa (Carrot Halwa)
cuisine: Indian | meal: dessert
- 5-6 | Carrots, grated | carrots
- 5 Tbsp | Milk powder | milk powder
- 1 tsp | Ghee | ghee
- 2 Tbsp | Golden raisins | golden raisins
- 1/4 cup | Sugar | sugar
- | Water | water
- 1/4 tsp | Cardamom powder | cardamom powder
> Heat ghee and cook raisins, stirring constantly, set aside.
> In a pan cook carrots till it releases water, add milk powder and toast till golden and slightly dry.
> Add sugar and cardamom powder, cook for few mins.
> Add water if needed and cook.
> Add raisins and turn off heat.
note: page 4; book title also says "by Swaad Anusar" (attribution, owner confirmed)
