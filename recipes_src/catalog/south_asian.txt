== Chicken Karahi
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: dinner | minutes: 45 | servings: 4 | img: chicken karahi
- 1 kg | Chicken, bone-in pieces | chicken thighs
- 4 | Tomatoes, chopped | tomatoes
- 1/2 cup | Cooking oil or ghee | vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp | Ginger-garlic paste | ginger garlic paste
- 4 | Green chilies | green chili
- 1 tsp | Cumin seeds | cumin
- 1 tsp | Coriander powder | ground coriander
- 1 tsp | Red chili powder | chili powder
- 1/2 tsp | Garam masala | garam masala
- | Salt | salt
- | Fresh coriander and ginger, to garnish | cilantro
> Heat the oil in a wok, add the cumin seeds and ginger-garlic paste and fry for a minute.
> Add the chicken and sear on high heat until it changes colour, about 8 minutes.
> Add the tomatoes, green chilies, salt, coriander and chili powder. Cover and cook on medium until the oil separates and the chicken is tender, 25 minutes.
> Finish with garam masala, ginger and fresh coriander.

== Chicken Biryani
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: dinner | minutes: 90 | servings: 6 | img: chicken biryani
- 3 cups | Basmati rice | basmati rice
- 1 kg | Chicken pieces | chicken thighs
- 2 cups | Plain yogurt | plain yogurt
- 3 | Onions, thinly sliced | onions
- 3 | Tomatoes, chopped | tomatoes
- 2 tbsp | Ginger-garlic paste | ginger garlic paste
- 2 tbsp | Biryani masala | biryani masala
- 1 tsp | Turmeric | turmeric
- 1/2 cup | Cooking oil | vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup | Mint leaves | mint
- 4 | Green chilies | green chili
- | Whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaf) | whole spices
- | Salt | salt
> Soak the rice 30 minutes. Fry the onions in oil until deep golden; set half aside for layering.
> Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, spices, yogurt and chicken to the pot. Cook until the chicken is tender and the masala is thick.
> Boil the rice in salted water with whole spices until 70% cooked, then drain.
> Layer rice over the chicken, top with fried onions, mint and chilies. Cover tightly and steam on low for 20 minutes.

== Beef Nihari
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: dinner | minutes: 180 | servings: 6 | img: nihari beef stew

- 1/2 cup | Cooking oil or ghee | ghee
- 2 | Onions, sliced | onions
- 3 tbsp | Nihari masala | nihari masala
- 3 tbsp | Whole wheat flour | whole wheat flour
- 1 tbsp | Ginger-garlic paste | ginger garlic paste
- | Fresh ginger strips, green chilies and lemon, to serve | ginger
- | Salt | salt
> Brown the sliced onions in ghee, add the meat and ginger-garlic paste and fry for 10 minutes.
> Add nihari masala, salt and 6 cups of water. Cover and simmer on low for 2.5 hours until the meat is falling apart.
