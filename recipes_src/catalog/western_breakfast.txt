== Fluffy Pancakes
cuisine: Western | meal: breakfast | minutes: 25 | servings: 4 | img: pancakes
- 1 1/2 cups | All-purpose flour | flour
- 2 tbsp | Sugar | sugar
- 1 tbsp | Baking powder | baking powder
- 1 1/4 cups | Milk | milk
- 1 | Egg | eggs
- 3 tbsp | Melted butter | butter
- | Maple syrup, to serve | maple syrup
> Whisk the dry ingredients, then stir in milk, egg and butter until just combined.
> Cook 1/4 cup scoops on a buttered griddle until bubbles form, flip and cook until golden.

- 500 g | Ground beef | ground beef

- 2 cups | Ricotta cheese | ricotta
- 3 cups | Shredded mozzarella | mozzarella
- 1 | Onion, chopped | onions
- 3 cloves | Garlic | garlic
- 1 | Egg | eggs
> Brown the beef with onion and garlic, add the sauce and simmer 15 minutes. Mix the ricotta with the egg.
> Layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta and mozzarella three times, ending with cheese.
