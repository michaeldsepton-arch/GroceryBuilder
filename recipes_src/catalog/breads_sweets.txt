== Naan
cuisine: Indian | meal: bread | minutes: 90 | servings: 8 | img: naan bread
- 3 cups | All-purpose flour | flour
- 1 tsp | Instant yeast | instant yeast
- 1 tsp | Sugar | sugar
- 1/2 cup | Plain yogurt | plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup | Warm milk | milk
- 2 tbsp | Oil or ghee | ghee
- 1 tsp | Salt | salt
> Mix everything into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes and rise 1 hour.
> Divide, roll into ovals and cook on a very hot dry pan or cast iron until puffed and charred. Brush with ghee.

== Roti (Chapati)
cuisine: Pakistani | meal: bread | minutes: 30 | servings: 8 | img: chapati roti
- 2 cups | Whole wheat flour (atta) | whole wheat flour
- 3/4 cup | Warm water | water
- 1/2 tsp | Salt | salt
> Knead the flour, water and salt into a soft dough and rest 20 minutes.
> Roll thin rounds and cook on a hot tawa until it puffs, flipping once.

== Cinnamon Rolls
cuisine: Western | meal: dessert | minutes: 120 | servings: 12 | img: cinnamon rolls
- 3 1/2 cups | Flour | flour
- 2 1/4 tsp | Instant yeast | instant yeast
- 1 cup | Warm milk | milk
- 1/3 cup | Sugar | sugar
- 1/3 cup | Butter, softened | butter
- 1 | Egg | eggs
- 1/2 cup | Brown sugar, for filling | brown sugar
- 2 tbsp | Cinnamon | cinnamon
- 4 oz | Cream cheese, for icing | cream cheese
> Make a soft dough from the flour, yeast, milk, sugar, butter and egg, knead and rise 1 hour.
> Roll into a rectangle, spread with butter, brown sugar and cinnamon, roll up and cut into 12.
> Rise 30 minutes, bake at 180°C for 25 minutes, and top with cream cheese icing.

== Baklava
cuisine: Turkish | meal: dessert | minutes: 90 | servings: 24 | img: baklava
- 1 package | Phyllo dough | phyllo dough
- 3 cups | Walnuts or pistachios, chopped | walnuts
- 1 cup | Butter, melted | butter
- 1 cup | Sugar | sugar
- 1 cup | Water | water
- 1/2 cup | Honey | honey
- 1 | Lemon, juiced | lemon juice
> Layer the phyllo in a pan, brushing each sheet with butter, scattering nuts between layers. Cut into diamonds.
> Bake at 175°C for 45 minutes. Boil the sugar, water, honey and lemon for 10 minutes and pour over the hot baklava.

- 3 cups | Mozzarella or akkawi cheese | mozzarella
- 1 1/2 cups | Sugar | sugar
- 3/4 cup | Water | water
- 1 tbsp | Rose water | rose water
- | Chopped pistachios | pistachios
