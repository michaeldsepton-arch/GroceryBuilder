== Chicken Kabsa
cuisine: Arab | meal: dinner | minutes: 75 | servings: 6 | img: kabsa
- 2 cups | Basmati rice | basmati rice
- 1 kg | Chicken pieces | chicken thighs
- 2 | Onions, chopped | onions
- 3 | Tomatoes, chopped | tomatoes
- 2 tbsp | Tomato paste | tomato paste
- 2 tbsp | Kabsa spice mix | kabsa spice
- 4 | Dried limes (loomi) | dried lime
- 1/4 cup | Oil or ghee | ghee
- 1/4 cup | Raisins and toasted almonds | almonds
- | Salt | salt
> Brown the chicken in ghee, remove, then fry the onions until golden.
> Add tomatoes, tomato paste, spices, dried limes and chicken with 4 cups of water. Simmer 30 minutes.
> Add the rinsed rice to the stock, cover and cook on low for 20 minutes. Top with raisins and almonds.
